CASE STUDY

Bringing A Global Mission Online

A brand-first website design for a non-profit finding its digital voice, built to make their mission easy to understand and easy to support.

Pain Points

THE CLIENT

World Wide Unified

World Wide Unified is an interfaith, global organization dedicated to fostering love and connection between people of all backgrounds and beliefs. Their mission is to create a world where everyone feels valued and connected.

Location
Remote
Launch Plan

Orbit

Project Type

New Website

Industry

Non-Profit

The Goal

Keep the familiar parts of the brand, especially the colors and typography from their logo, while giving the website a fresh, modern feel. We wanted it to feel more engaging without losing the brand’s identity, while making it easier for visitors to find what they need, understand World Wide Unified’s vision, and navigate the site comfortably.

The Approach

Through user research, we found that most of the nonprofit’s audience was older adults. Because of that, we wanted to make the site as easy and accessible to use as possible while still keeping the brand’s existing look and feel. We paid close attention to the navigation, typography, and layout, especially on mobile, to make sure the site felt simple and easy to get around

The Result

The final design gave World Wide Unified a fresh, approachable website while staying true to the brand they had already built. We focused on clear navigation, accessible typography, and a simple mobile experience that would be easy for their audience to use. While the site ultimately wasn’t launched due to changes in the organization’s funding, the project allowed us to create a complete digital experience built around their goals and audience.
